Usage-based AI exposure across Australia's earnings distribution

Mapping US task-usage data onto ANZSCO occupations

Luc Taya, Economics (Honours) graduate, University of Western Australia

Working paper v1.0, comments welcome

Descriptive; not a job-loss or career-risk score; not advice.

The data behind this work are task-level Claude.ai and first-party API use by self-selected users of one provider, classified to US occupational tasks. No Australian occupational AI use is measured. The usage-based scores describe tasks in US occupations and are applied to the occupations Australians work in, then compared with measures of AI potential that do not come from Anthropic.
