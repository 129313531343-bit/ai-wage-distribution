## Anthropic Economic Index

Anthropic Economic Index dataset (Hugging Face, Anthropic/EconomicIndex, commit `4e5f69f`). The dataset card states "Data released under CC-BY" and gives no version. Massenkoff, M. and McCrory, P. (2026), "Labor market impacts of AI: A new measure and early evidence".

## Eloundou et al.

Eloundou, T., Manning, S., Mishkin, P. and Rock, D. (2024), "GPTs are GPTs: Labor market impact potential of LLMs", *Science* 384: 1306-1308. Occupation data from https://github.com/openai/GPTs-are-GPTs (MIT licence).

## Felten, Raj and Seamans (AIOE)

Felten, E., Raj, M. and Seamans, R. AIOE data from https://github.com/AIOE-Data/AIOE. The repository states no licence. The data are used only in aggregate charts and correlations, and no per-occupation values are published.
