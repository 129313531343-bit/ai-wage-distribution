Usage-based AI exposure by Australian occupation

Explorer for the working draft "Usage-based AI exposure across Australia's earnings distribution"

Descriptive; not a job-loss or career-risk score; not advice.

The usage-based scores come from task-level Claude.ai and first-party API use by self-selected users of one provider, classified to US occupational tasks, mapped from US occupations to ANZSCO unit groups. No Australian occupational AI use is measured. Each unit also shows potential-exposure measures that do not come from Anthropic (Jobs and Skills Australia; Eloundou et al.). A score of 0.00 marked "below usage threshold" means the occupation's tasks fell below the source's usage gate; it does not mean no use. See the note for methods and limitations.
